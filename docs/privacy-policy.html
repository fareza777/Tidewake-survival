Tidewake: Survival RPG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Tidewake: Survival RPG is an offline-first game. This policy explains the limited data used when optional advertising and the one-time Remove Ads purchase are enabled.

Information we use

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Saved games and settings stay on your device in browser storage or Android preferences.
  • The Android build may show Google AdMob banner ads. AdMob may process device identifiers, coarse location and ad interaction data to deliver, measure and prevent fraud on ads. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy and the consent choices shown in the app.
  • The optional one-time Remove Ads purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive the store transaction result needed to acknowledge the purchase and keep the entitlement on the device; we do not receive payment card details.

Network use

The game can be played offline. Sharing and ratings open the Android or Google Play surfaces you choose.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
