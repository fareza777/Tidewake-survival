# Google Play listing (English)

**App name (30):** Tidewake: Island Survival

**Short description (80):** Survive a mysterious island: craft, build, fight bosses and find a way home.

**Full description (4000):**

Washed ashore after a storm, you have nothing but the clothes you wear. Tidewake: Island Survival is an offline pixel-art survival adventure: gather, craft, build a camp, cook, farm, hunt and explore a whole island that is different every time you play.

SURVIVE
- Keep hunger, thirst and stamina up while a real day and night pass (a day lasts ten minutes).
- Chop, mine and harvest with tools that wear out and get better: wood, stone and iron.
- Build a campfire, a workbench, a furnace, a bed, chests, an alchemy table and more. Light the night with fires, torches and lamp posts.
- More than 60 recipes: tools, weapons, armor, meals, potions and furniture.
- Plant and water crops, catch fish, hunt animals and cook what you find.

EXPLORE
- A new island from every seed: forest, mountain, swamp and desert, with hidden bottles, ancient tablets and buried treasure.
- Three dungeons full of push blocks, floor switches, spike traps, crystals and locked chests, each ending in a boss with two phases.
- A final fight at the lighthouse, and two endings: sail home, or stay and light the lighthouse again.

MEET THE ISLANDERS
- Five islanders with stories to tell: a ten-chapter main quest and ten side quests.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Three difficulties (Relaxed, Normal, Hardcore) and three save slots.
- Works fully offline. No ads. No purchases. English and Bahasa Indonesia.

Pixel art: Super Retro Collection by Gif.
